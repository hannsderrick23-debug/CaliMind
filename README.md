## What CaliMind does

CaliMind brings task capture, daily planning, and focused work together in one
mobile app. Add tasks by speaking or typing, organize them around your day, and
adjust your plan when priorities change.

## Features

- **Voice task capture** — speak naturally, review the interpreted task, and
  confirm it before it is added.
- **Flexible task management** — create, edit, complete, and organize tasks by
  focus category, due date, priority, and recurrence.
- **Daily scheduling** — generate a proposed schedule, review it, and decide
  what to save. Replan remaining work without moving completed tasks.
- **Calendar-aware planning** — optionally account for busy calendar intervals
  on supported platforms.
- **Focus sessions** — work on one task with a built-in focus timer.
- **Progress insights** — see weekly task progress and build a clearer picture
  of your follow-through.
- **Reminders and notifications** — get timely prompts about tasks and plans.
- **Secure sign-in** — account access, password recovery, and device biometric
  unlock where supported.
- **Home-screen widget support** — see useful task information from your device
  home screen where supported.
- **Accessible, responsive interface** — a calm, purple-led design for
  everyday planning.

## How to use

### Get CaliMind

Install the latest published Android build from the
[CaliMind releases](https://github.com/hannsderrick23-debug/CaliMind/releases/latest).
The app is also available to run locally with Flutter:

```sh
flutter pub get
flutter run
```

### Plan your day

1. Create an account or sign in.
2. Add a task with the microphone or the task form. Review voice-captured
   details and confirm before saving.
3. Choose a focus category and add useful details such as priority, duration,
   due date, or a repeat schedule.
4. Open **Schedule** and generate a proposed plan. Review the suggested time
   slots and unscheduled tasks before saving.
5. Start a focus session from a task, then check your weekly progress as you
   complete work.
6. Adjust notification, calendar, security, and profile preferences in
   **Settings**.

## Why use CaliMind

CaliMind is designed to make planning feel more manageable—not to fill every
minute. Voice capture makes it easier to get tasks out of your head, schedule
review keeps changes in your hands, and focus sessions help turn a busy list
into one clear next step.
